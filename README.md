# My first repository

## My Favorite Anime: *Masamune-kun's Revenge*

> "I will make her fall for me, and then break her heart." — Masamune Makabe

**Masamune-kun's Revenge** is a romantic comedy anime about a boy who was cruelly rejected as a child and vows to take *revenge* on the girl responsible. ~~It is just another romance~~ It is a *hilarious* and **heartwarming** story with a great twist.

---

### Quick Facts

| Detail      | Info                                  |
|-------------|---------------------------------------|
| Genre       | Romance, Comedy, Drama                |
| Studio      | Silver Link                           |
| Original By | Hazuki Takeoka (light novel)          |
| Seasons     | Season 1 (2017), Season 2 "R" (2023)  |

### Main Characters

1. Masamune Makabe
2. Aki Adagaki
3. Yoshino Koiwai
4. Neko Kobayakawa

### Why I Like It

- Funny and clever plot
- Great character development
- Memorable character moments
- Enjoyable mix of comedy and romance
